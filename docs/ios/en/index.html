Privacy policy

This policy explains how Dalili fi al-Islam on iOS and iPadOS handles your data, and how this public privacy and support website works.

01Data stored by the app

Dalili fi al-Islam works primarily offline. Your language, theme, reading mode, text size and presentation preferences, together with your favorites, last reading position, reading positions and adhkar progress, are stored in the app’s private space on your device.

The app does not require a user account and does not synchronize this information with a developer-operated server. The developer does not receive your favorites, progress, searches or reading choices. The app does not add these local data to advertising requests.

02Location, prayer times and qibla

With your iOS or iPadOS permission, the app uses your precise or approximate location while in use to calculate prayer times and the qibla direction. You can refuse or withdraw this permission in your device settings.

The selected coordinates, city name, time zone and calculation method are stored locally. Calculations and compass use take place on your device. The app does not request background location access.

If the city name is not found in the included list, the app may ask Apple to find it from your coordinates. This geocoding may require Internet access and send your location to Apple’s service under its privacy policy. The app does not add your coordinates to AdMob requests.

03Backup and changing devices

The app has no developer-operated backup or synchronization service. Depending on your settings, Apple may include app data in an iCloud backup or a transfer to a new device. You can manage backups in Apple settings.

A normal update preserves your preferences and progress. The developer has no copy from which to recover them remotely; any restoration depends on the backups you have kept.

04Advertising and Google services

Dalili fi al-Islam uses Google AdMob to display banners on selected screens, and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage advertising privacy choices.

According to Google, the advertising SDK may process IP addresses, device or app identifiers, interactions with ads and the app, and diagnostic and performance data. These data support advertising, measurement and fraud prevention.

UMP processes information needed to present and record your choices. Ad requests depend on the authorization status supplied by UMP. These processes are separate from the favorites, progress and coordinates stored locally by the app.

05Permissions and choices

You can manage location and notification permissions in iOS or iPadOS settings. Prayer reminders are optional and use local notifications. Their content and scheduling are prepared on your device.

A privacy form is displayed when UMP requires it. The “Change your Google UMP consent” option in More lets you revisit the available choices when required.

The current version does not request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission. Access to the IDFA advertising identifier remains subject to iOS protections; the Google SDK may use other app- or developer-bounded identifiers. Refusing personalization does not necessarily stop technical processing for permitted ads.

06Retention and deletion

Preferences, favorites, reading positions, progress and configured coordinates are stored in the app’s private space. To erase these data from your device, use “Delete App” in iOS or iPadOS storage settings. Offloading the app while keeping its data does not erase them.

A copy may remain in your Apple backups: manage those copies separately in the relevant settings.

The developer cannot view or remotely delete local data. Google and Apple apply their own retention and deletion rules, described in their policies.

07Data protection

Local data benefit from the protections of the app’s private space on iOS and iPadOS. Communications needed for advertising and geocoding rely on secure connections provided by Google and Apple services and on iOS network protections.

No technical measure guarantees absolute security. Protect access to your device and install available security updates.

08Support, contact and privacy

For help, to report a problem with the app, or to contact Aghiles Tech about privacy and the rights applicable to your situation: aghitech92@gmail.com.

Please include whether you use an iPhone or iPad, your app version and the problem you encountered. If you email us, your address and message are processed to answer your request and kept for as long as needed to follow it up, subject to legal obligations. Send only information relevant to your request.

09Visiting this website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains no ads, audience analytics, forms, scripts or cookies added by the developer. GitHub receives technical information needed to host the site, including IP addresses and usage data, and may keep logs to provide and secure its service. Its privacy statement is linked below.

External websites opened from this page apply their own policies.

10Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if features, SDKs or data practices change. The date at the top identifies the current version. The French, English and Arabic versions of this page describe the same iOS and iPadOS practices. The Android policy is available through the platform selector.
